Safari Zoo Drive

Pick a continent and climb into your jeep. Find wild animals, jump out, catch them in your net, put them in the cages on your trailer and take them back to your zoo.

🪙 0
Drive
WASD or arrow keys. Drag the mouse (or your finger) to look around.
Jump out
Stop near an animal and press E. Press Space to jump. Press E by the jeep to get back in.
Catch
Look at the animal and press F to throw your net. Rare animals run away, so wait until they get tired!
Cage it
Lead the animal back to the cages on your trailer and press E. Then drive it to your zoo.
