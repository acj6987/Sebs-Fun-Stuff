Safari Zoo Drive

Pick a continent, or Dino Island, and climb into your safari truck. Find wild animals, jump out, catch them in your net, put them in the 8 cages on your trailer and take them back to your zoo.

🪙 0
Drive
WASD or arrow keys. Drag the mouse (or your finger) to look around.
Jump out
Stop near an animal and press E. Press Space to jump, and R to ride your pet Triceratops or Velociraptor.
Catch
Look at the animal and press F to throw your net. Rare animals run away, so wait until they get tired!
Your zoo
Bring caged animals home and put them in big glass domes. Then drive your truck around the zoo and right inside the domes.

Which enclosure?

Pick a number. Your truck starts inside that dome so you can drive around with the animals.

Your animals

Every animal you bring home goes straight into the dome for its home. Big cats and zebras live in the Savanna dome, penguins in the Polar dome, and so on. Tap a dome to see who lives there.

Build a new dome

A dome is built for free when you catch an animal from a new home. You can also buy an extra one.
