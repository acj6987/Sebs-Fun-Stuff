How long one episode takes to make

A single Pokémon episode is about 22 minutes of finished cartoon, and it is made by the animation studio OLM. It is not made all at once — it moves down a production line, and each stage is a different team of people.

    Altogether one episode takes roughly a few months from the first script to the finished film, and the drawing and painting part alone is usually about a month of work. The studio can still release one episode every week because around 8 to 10 episodes are being worked on at the same time, each at a different stage — like a conveyor belt.
