The road

Pelogoni

Some places you visit. Pelogoni you came back from carrying something. One road, two brothers, and a trip that turned into a story you each tell slightly differently — and somehow you are both right.

The years before all this

School days

Same classroom, same register, the same handwriting on a good day. Teachers who never quite worked out which of you was which — and two of you in absolutely no hurry to help them.

Thirty-nine looks good on both of you. Here's to the next thirty-nine — same day, same cake, same trouble.
