HOW TO PLAY

Build your bey from a Blade (power), Ratchet (burst lock & height) and Bit (movement style).

Launch: press SPACE (P2: ENTER) to stop the power meter — the closer to the top, the stronger your shot.

Battle plays out physically. When your special gauge is full, press SPACE (P2: ENTER) or tap your side of the screen to fire your type's special move.

Xtreme rail: aggressive flat-bit beys that hit the glowing outer rail with speed lock on, accelerate, and slingshot across the stadium for massive damage.

SPIN FINISH +1 outlast their spin  ·  OVER FINISH +2 knock them into a side pocket  ·  BURST FINISH +2 break their bey apart  ·  XTREME FINISH +3 smash them out through the X pocket. First to 4 points wins. Win VS CPU matches to unlock new blades!
