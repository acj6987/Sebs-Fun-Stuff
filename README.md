# Spin Blade World

A 3D spinning-top battle game made for Seb, Freddie and Josh.

- Walk around the city in 3D
- **Blade Shop**: buy new blades and bits
- **Design Lab**: pick your blade and bit, paint it, and give it a name
- **Practice Dome**: friendly battles (or walk up to your friends and challenge them)
- **X Tournament Tower**: Neighbourhood Cup → City Championship → National X League → X World Championship
- **Hall of Champs**: your trophies and stats

The crew: **Seb** (Team Leader, Raptor Strike), **Freddie** (Titanic Boa), **Josh** (Spinosaurus).

## How to play
Open `index.html` in a browser (it needs internet to load the 3D library).

- Walk: WASD / arrow keys (on a phone or tablet: the on-screen joystick)
- Enter a building or talk to someone: E, Enter or Space (on a phone or tablet: the yellow button)
- Battle: press LAUNCH when the power bar is full, steer your blade, and press SPACE (or DASH) to smash into your opponent

First to 4 points wins: Spin Finish 1 · Over Finish 2 · Burst Finish 2 · Xtreme Finish 3.
